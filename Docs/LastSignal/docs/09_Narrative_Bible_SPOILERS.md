# Narrative bible and spoilers

## Dramatic premise

Eli Vale follows his wife Mara along the Greywater evacuation route after their convoy separated during a collapse at a road barrier. He believes her voice is still coming through the relay network. Mara coordinated transport and medical supplies; her trail contains decisions she made for other people, not only clues left for her husband.

The question begins as Where is she and becomes When did this happen. The final answer is sad but concrete: Mara died helping evacuate people at North Relay before Eli's present journey began. Several messages he hears are automated rebroadcasts. His stress and guilt affect interpretation, while the physical evidence remains consistent. Do not imply that mental illness makes him violent or that every event was imaginary.

This ending is a proposed creative default, clearly separated from technical contracts. It can be changed before final clue recording, provided the timeline and evidence are rewritten together.

## Objective timeline

Day 0: the convoy splits; Mara continues north with a relief group. Eli is injured and loses reliable time references. Day 1: Mara passes Mile Zero and marks a map. Day 3: she reaches Redline Depot and leaves a dispatch note. Day 5: she rests at Stillwater House and repairs her coat. Day 6: she reaches North Relay and helps route an evacuation. Day 7: she dies during a rescue, after recording a personal message. Day 10: Eli resumes the route. Fixed-date records expose the delay; the UI never falsely labels an old recording as live.

## First three regions

Region 1, km 0-12: practical hope. Mile Zero contains the marked map and a familiar cup. At km 4, the road log confirms she travelled through. At km 8, an object suggests a deliberate detour. A radio sign-off repeats with the same small click, initially easy to dismiss. The first garage tutorial is motivated by preparing the car she helped maintain.

Region 2, km 12-30: contradictory chronology. Redline's handwritten dispatch book shows Day 3, while a damaged printed receipt uses an earlier date. Investigation explains that the printer clock was wrong rather than relying on a supernatural trick. At km 18, a witness note mentions her blue coat and another traveller. At km 24, the route stamp proves her stop occurred days before Eli expected. Replayed radio phrasing becomes recognisable. The player should begin questioning Eli's assumptions, not the game's honesty.

Region 3, km 30-48: intimacy and acceptance. Stillwater looks inhabited because an absent caretaker prepared it for future arrivals. Mara's repaired coat and recording instructions reveal that messages were meant to repeat. At km 36, a relief manifest names people she helped. At km 42, the final personal recording is recovered but its damaged last segment cannot play until the relay is reached. At km 48, an objective log and a named survivor's written testimony confirm her death. Her complete message asks Eli to continue living, without magically knowing his exact future route.

## Finale and continuation

The final driving leg culminates in reaching the relay through a visible evacuation choke, not an unrelated boss arena. Inside a small safe room, the player restores power and hears the complete recording, with subtitles and a skippable transcript. No timed combat interrupts it.

Choose whether to leave the repaired radio at Stillwater or keep it in the car. This affects one home prop and a quiet epilogue line, not a paid item or contradictory ending. The campaign ends clearly. Endless mode is a post-story continuation of Eli maintaining supply routes; the search narrative does not keep pretending Mara is alive.

Clues are guaranteed and journalled once; optional echoes can vary. Use no more than one overtly uncanny beat per major leg: a repeated radio click, an object remembered in the wrong place, or a reflection that resolves on a second look. Such moments never alter collision, hide essential hazards or change already recorded evidence. Avoid jump-scare spam and the reveal that the whole journey never happened.

## Writing and delivery style

Short practical notes with personal details beat exposition. Mara's voice is competent, tired and caring, without saintly perfection. Eli speaks sparingly; breathing, hesitation and the car's familiar sounds carry much of the emotion. Write evidence text before recording dialogue. Maintain a clue timeline spreadsheet or JSON and review every revision for date consistency and accidental spoilers in filenames exposed to players.
