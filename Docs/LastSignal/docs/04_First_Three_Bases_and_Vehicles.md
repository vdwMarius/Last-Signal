# First three bases vehicles and upgrades

All names and dimensions are proposed design specifications. Build original forms without real vehicle badges or copied game landmarks. Dimensions use metres; Unreal uses centimetres after verified import. Vehicle figures are gameplay tuning seeds, not claims about real vehicles. Major vehicle rewards are free and permanent on first checkpoint completion.

## G01 Mile Zero Service

Purpose: a small, credible shelter that teaches working on a vehicle and makes the first departure emotionally clear. A shuttered roadside repair shop has been barricaded by its last owner. Cold daylight falls through dirty glass; one amber work lamp lights the wheel bay. Mara's marked road map lies near a chipped blue enamel cup.

Blockout: workshop 14 x 10 m with a 5 m clear height, rear living room 6 x 5 m, fenced forecourt 18 x 16 m. Single 4 x 3.5 m vehicle door; 1.2 m clear walking paths and a protected service clearance around the parked car. The 1.8 m player reference must reach the bench and wheel without crouching.

Stations: jack and wheel rack beside the bay; scrap bench at the rear wall; route board beside the exit; radio and bed in the living room; paint rack unlocked by the tutorial. Decoration zones: living-room floor, two walls, shelves and a small forecourt strip. Do not place furniture in the service bay.

Asset set: corrugated roof, plaster/brick wall modules, shutter, office partition, two windows, workbench, hydraulic jack, tyre rack, route board, radio, cot, cup, fuel can, toolbox, fluorescent fixture and fence kit. Reuse the tools in later garages. Story focal point is the map and cup, never a large exposition wall.

Concept prompt: original game environment, abandoned inland roadside service shop, modest corrugated roof and worn plaster brick, a single believable vehicle bay, oil stains and restrained repair clutter, cold overcast daylight with one warm task lamp, quiet refuge inside a dark zombie evacuation world, realistic PBR surfaces, desaturated blue-grey and rust, readable walkways, no people, no logos, no text, 16:9 eye-level interior. Produce a separate top-down layout study using the stated dimensions; technical dimensions remain authoritative if the generated image disagrees.

## V01 Kestrel estate wagon

Role: light, economical, nimble starter. Long roof, narrow upright headlights, slightly squared wheel arches, repaired rear quarter and a blank grille make a recognisable silhouette without copying a manufacturer. A faded off-white body has one slate-blue replacement door. Interior: cloth seats, worn analogue gauges, a taped photograph recess and an empty radio cradle.

Dimensions: 4.45 x 1.75 x 1.48 m; 2.62 m wheelbase; 0.31 m wheel radius. Four wheels; modest ground clearance. Seed mass 1280 kg, top speed 110 km/h, fuel capacity 18 game-units, nominal range 4.5 km, cargo 8 slots, body integrity 100. Fuel is deliberately compressed for the arcade loop and is not litres.

## G02 Redline Depot

Purpose: the first feeling of strength and ownership, with enough space to compare two vehicles. A deserted municipal transport workshop sits beside railway sidings and a disused loading platform. Red oxide beams and dirty skylights frame a sunken inspection pit. A small upstairs dispatch room overlooks the bay.

Blockout: main hall 24 x 16 m, clear height 7 m; dispatch room 8 x 5 m; fenced yard 30 x 22 m. One active vehicle pad and one static display pad keep physics and UI simple. Stairs are 1.4 m wide. Guard the inspection pit with a removable service cover and safe navigation collision.

Stations: powered bench, parts shelves, paint corner with ventilation fan, route board in dispatch, bed alcove and salvage locker. Functional unlocks are higher recipe tiers and vehicle selection, not damage buffs from furniture. Decoration zones: dispatch, lounge, marked walls and courtyard pockets.

Asset set: industrial truss, brick infill, skylight, pit module, stairs/rail, roller door, depot gantry as static scenery, lockers, oil drums, tool trolley and dispatch furniture. Build at least 70% of walls/props from common kits. Distinct story props: Mara's luggage tag and a dispatch ledger with two contradictory dates, rendered as authored readable UI when inspected.

Concept prompt: original abandoned transport depot converted into a survivor workshop, red oxide roof beams, worn masonry, dirty skylight, single active car bay with covered inspection pit, raised dispatch office, organised salvaged tools, restrained amber work lighting in cold industrial dusk, dark quiet mood, plausible architecture, clear circulation, no logos or generated lettering, 16:9 wide interior.

## G03 Stillwater House

Purpose: a refuge that feels almost like a home, making the final journey harder to leave. A weatherboard/concrete reservoir caretaker house stands above a drained inlet. Its attached boathouse has become a workshop. The rooms feel carefully maintained, although no one answers the radio.

Blockout: house 12 x 10 m, garage 16 x 12 m with 6 m clearance, covered passage 4 x 6 m and fenced lookout deck 8 x 5 m. No accessible lake, boat simulation or large explorable estate. Workshop clearance accommodates the Warden. Use horizon scenery to imply a larger place.

Stations: universal parts bench, paint wall, route desk facing the reservoir, bedroom, kitchen and radio nook. Decoration focus: books, lamps, rugs, collected objects and a small memorial shelf. The initial composition supports both comfort and unease. Daylight stays cold; personal lamps warm the player-built interior.

Asset set: house wall/door/window kit, concrete plinth, pitched roof, timber deck, workshop doors, simple kitchen, sofa, desk, curtains, lamps, railings and reservoir vista cards/meshes. Hero story props: repaired coat, taped radio cassette and an identifiable scratch on the desk. Furniture reuses material families from G01/G02.

Concept prompt: original secluded reservoir caretaker house with attached high workshop, weatherboard above concrete foundations, mist over a receding lake, practical lived-in rooms with warm task lamps against cold blue-grey daylight, somber refuge, restrained repairs and human traces, no fantasy castle, no luxury mansion, no people or text, cinematic yet playable architectural proportions. Generate garage interior and home interior separately.

## Common optional specialisations

All cars can unlock a 700-scrap Economy Tune (+12% nominal range, -8% engine force), an 800-scrap Reinforced Shell (+20% body integrity, +8% mass) and a 900-scrap Quick Steering package (+10% steering response with reduced high-speed assistance). These are alternative compatible parts in engine, ram and suspension slots respectively, not extra stacking slots. The tuning evaluator must include each tradeoff in preview text. Prices and effects require playtesting.
